The dataset is stored on a Onedata research-data platform, in {DATASET_LOCATION}. In your sandbox, `fs = {FS_EXPR}` gives a preconfigured, authenticated fsspec filesystem for the platform. `fs.get(remote_path, local_path)` downloads a whole file to local disk under `{SCRATCH}`; files cannot be read remotely in place, so open the local copy with xarray (for example `xarray.open_dataset(path, engine="h5netcdf")`).
